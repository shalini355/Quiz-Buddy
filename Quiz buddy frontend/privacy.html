Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Educational Project

Quiz Buddy values your trust and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy provides a clear and transparent explanation of how data is handled across our educational quiz application.

1. Information We Collect

Quiz Buddy operates primarily as a client-side web application. We collect minimal information strictly required to facilitate quiz practice:

  • Guest Mode Data: No personal identity data is required or collected. A temporary identifier (e.g. "Guest Coder") is maintained locally on your device.
  • Registered Account Data: If you choose to create an account, your username and email are stored locally in your browser's Web Storage (localStorage) to personalize your experience.
  • Quiz Performance Data: Your quiz answers, scores, timestamps, and badges are stored on your device to display your personal dashboard and history.

2. How Your Information is Stored

All session and quiz history data is held directly within your browser's local storage. We do not transmit or sell your data to third-party brokers, advertisers, or behavioral trackers.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Quiz Buddy does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, fingerprinting scripts, or persistent cross-site trackers. We rely on standard browser storage mechanisms to save your in-progress quizzes and theme preferences.

4. Your Rights and Data Control

Because your data is stored locally in your browser, you retain complete ownership. You can clear your quiz scores, guest sessions, or stored credentials at any time by clearing your browser cache and local storage, or by selecting "Reset Data" in the user settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Quiz Buddy's data practices, please reach out via our Contact Page.
